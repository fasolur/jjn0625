Lucide
Minimalist Markers

An open-source icon library hand-crafted on a 24×24 grid. Consistent stroke weight, generous whitespace, and a single unified design language — every glyph knows exactly what it is.

Open & Contributed

Maintained by a small community of designers and developers since 2022, under the ISC license.

24×24 Grid System

Every icon is drawn on the same invisible canvas, so they sit together in quiet alignment.

Pure SVG

No web fonts, no ligatures, no fallback stacks — just crisp, editable, infinitely scalable vectors.
